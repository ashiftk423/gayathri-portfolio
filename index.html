01 — About

Apps that stay clear once the real work starts.

I am a Flutter and web developer in Thrissur. I care about interfaces that stay understandable when the data shows up — logins, roles, lists that grow, and statuses people check all day.

At Astrins Technology I build cross-platform apps with Clean Architecture and BLoC, and I integrate REST APIs with token-based authentication and role-based access. Recent work includes Ksetra Printing, an internal order and status module, and a car parking system for entry, exit, and slot allocation.

Internships at Eqsoft and IROHUB put me on both sides of the stack: C# and SQL Server for a house rental product, and Python for backend modules. I sit with the brief, stay with the bug, and ship the calmer version.
